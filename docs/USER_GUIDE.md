# ShopStock — Quick User Guide (for shopkeepers)

1. **Start**: double-click the ShopStock icon on the Desktop. A black window opens (keep it open) and your browser shows ShopStock. To stop, close the black window.
2. **First time**: log in as `admin` / `admin123`, then choose your own password. Open **Settings** and fill in shop name, phone, address (they print on invoices).
3. **Add products**: Products → Add product (name, purchase price, selling price, opening stock, minimum stock). Barcode is optional.
4. **Add suppliers & record purchases**: when new stock arrives → Purchases → Record purchase. Stock increases automatically.
5. **Selling**: POS. Search a product (or scan barcode + Enter), add, set discount, choose payment method, *Complete sale*. For udhaar choose *Credit/Udhaar* and select the customer.
6. **Receipts**: on the invoice page — Download PDF (English), **اردو PDF**, Print Invoice, Thermal receipt, or Send on WhatsApp. To make Urdu the default invoice language: Settings → Invoice language.
7. **Customer pays udhaar**: Khata → Record payment. Send reminders with the 💬 button.
8. **Mistakes**: wrong sale → open it → *Void sale*. Customer brings an item back → *Return items*.
9. **Every day**: check the Dashboard (profit, low stock). **Backups are automatic**, but copy one to a USB drive every week (Backup & Restore → Download backup).
10. **Cashiers**: Users → Add user → role *Cashier*. They can sell but cannot see profit or cost prices.
